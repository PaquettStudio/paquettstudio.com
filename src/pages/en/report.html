Help us protect our games

Report a copy

Seen one of our games, or something pretending to be one, somewhere it shouldn’t be? Thank you for letting us know.

What to include

  1. The link to the listing, website or post.
  2. The app name and developer name, exactly as they are shown.
  3. For Google Play, the package ID: the part after id= in the link.
  4. A few screenshots and the date you found it.

Email the report

Or write to {{ site.company.emailLegal }}.

What happens next

  • We aim to confirm we’ve received your report within 5 working days.
  • We check it, keep the evidence and file a takedown request with the store or platform involved.
  • We only use your details to follow up on the report, and we don’t share them without asking you first.

You can also flag the listing directly in the store, using its own “Report” or “Flag as inappropriate” option. The official versions of our games are listed on our Copyright & IP page.
