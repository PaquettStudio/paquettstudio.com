We handle personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. This website

  • No cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We do not use cookies, analytics tools or third-party trackers on this website, and our fonts are served from our own site.
  • Preferences on your device. If you choose a language or turn sound effects on, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

3. When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to tell us. We use this only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages and running our business. Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which processes it on our behalf.

4. Our games

Abyss Angler™ has its own detailed privacy policy, which explains exactly what the game does with information and applies to the game: Abyss Angler privacy policy. In short:

  • The game never asks for your email address, phone number, location or contacts.
  • It sends anonymous usage counts (for example “a player reached 500 m”) linked only to a random identifier created on your device. You can switch them off in the game under Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics.
  • An account is created only if you tap “Keep a copy of my progress”. It holds a random player ID, a friend code, your captain name and portrait and a summary of your play, and you can delete it from inside the game.
  • Our game server runs on Cloudflare, acting as our processor. We do not store your IP address; we keep only the two-letter country code of an account.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional reward adverts are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). They are personalised only if you say yes when the game asks.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

When you download our games, Apple or Google process your download and purchase data under their own privacy policies, as independent controllers.
