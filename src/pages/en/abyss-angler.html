About the game

Into the deep

You start on a life ring with a short line and a single hook. Steer it down through the dark by hand, chase the shape you want, and start reeling before the sharks notice what you are carrying. Sell the catch, buy more line, go deeper.

Over 280 species wait between the sunlit shallows and the Forgotten Sea, more than 2,500 metres down. Build your way from a life ring to a sport yacht, ride out storms for rarer fish, crack open chests from the deep, and let your crew keep fishing while you are away.

It plays offline. An account is optional, and so are the reward adverts.
